# Privacy Policy

Cairn is a local-first application. This means:

## What we collect

Nothing. Cairn does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or any third party.

## What stays on your device

- Your project files
- Your settings
- Your GitHub token (encrypted with your OS keychain)
- Your session history

## Network activity

Cairn only makes network requests in two cases:

1. **Local network discovery (mDNS)** — to find teammates on the same WiFi
2. **GitHub API** — using your own token, only when you explicitly trigger a PR export

We do not have a server. We cannot see your code, your data, or your activity.

## Third-party services

When you use the "Save to GitHub" feature, you interact directly with GitHub's API under your own account and GitHub's terms.
