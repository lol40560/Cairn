## Your Projects and Credentials

You control the project folders, room credentials, network addresses, and GitHub credentials that you provide to Cairn. Before sharing a project or exporting content, confirm that you are authorized to share the files and that the selected recipients and repository are correct. Keep independent backups of important work; checkpoints and deleted-file recovery are convenience features, not a replacement for backups or version control.

## Peer-to-Peer Collaboration

When you create or join a room, Cairn sends the collaboration data required to synchronize the selected project directly to authenticated peers. Anyone with authorized access to that room may receive project content and change history. Protect room credentials and use collaboration only with people and devices you trust.
